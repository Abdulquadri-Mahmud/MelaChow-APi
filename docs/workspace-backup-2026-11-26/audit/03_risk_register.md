# Risk Register (Top 10 Ranked by Real-World Business Impact)

| Rank | Finding ID | Risk Summary | Environment | Plain-Language Business Consequence |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **F-01** | Infinite free wallet credit via `GET /api/wallet/verify/:reference` | **LIVE PROD** | Any customer can replay their own food-order payment reference—or share one top-up reference across multiple accounts—to print unlimited free MelaChow wallet balance and order food for free until discovered. |
| **2** | **F-02** | Super-admin MFA, lockout & rate-limit bypass via `POST /api/admin/login` | **BOTH** | Anyone who obtains or brute-forces a super-admin password logs straight into the highest-privilege account without email OTP, gaining control over payouts, admin accounts, and vendor bank details. |
| **3** | **F-03** | Unauthenticated vendor creation with mass assignment (`POST /api/vendors/create`) | **LIVE PROD** | Any outsider on the internet can create a pre-approved, live restaurant account with arbitrary payout settings, bypassing OTP verification and admin vetting. |
| **4** | **F-04** | Unauthenticated customer account takeover (`POST /api/user/auth/set-password`) | **BOTH** | Anyone knowing the email of a verified customer without a password (legacy OTP users or users mid-signup) can set a new password, take over the account, and spend their wallet balance. |
| **5** | **F-07** | PostgreSQL staging cutover breaks vendor signup, WebSockets, wallet top-up, bank setup, rider reset, and workers | **STAGING** | Promoting staging to production as-is will immediately break new vendor onboarding (500 errors), real-time order tracking/alerts (Socket.IO), customer wallet top-ups, and rider password resets. |
| **6** | **F-06** | Vendor bank-account lock bypass (`DELETE /api/wallet/bank-account`) | **LIVE PROD** | A compromised vendor account can delete its locked bank account and register an attacker's bank account to steal pending vendor payouts, defeating the fraud-prevention lock. |
| **7** | **F-05** | Unapproved vendor bypasses admin approval via Forgot Password | **BOTH** | Rejected or pending vendors can obtain a live vendor session via password reset and access authenticated vendor APIs without ever being approved by an admin. |
| **8** | **F-08** | Cross-customer order PII exposure via `POST /api/orders/v2/verify/:reference` | **BOTH** | Any logged-in customer with another customer's payment reference can read that customer's delivery address, phone number, and order details (NDPR privacy breach). |
| **9** | **F-09 & F-10** | Admin sub-role privilege escalation + unrevoked 30-day refresh tokens | **BOTH** | Standard ops admins can force-fail withdrawals and credit wallets without super-admin/finance-admin authority; stolen refresh cookies remain valid for 30 days even after logout. |
| **10** | **F-11 & F-14** | Live Paystack/Mongo credentials in Git history + 54 backend CVEs | **BOTH** | Anyone with repository read access can extract historical live Paystack/Mongo credentials (if unrotated); unpatched `mongoose`, `proxy-addr`, `express-rate-limit`, and `multer` expose production to DoS and rate-limit bypasses. |
